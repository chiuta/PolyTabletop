Ages and Monuments

Screenshots and clip

Rules in short

Original variant: 5 dice, up to 3 throws. Labour, food, goods, coins, skull (cannot be re-rolled; 2 skulls = −1, 3 = lose workers). 6 workers = monument (+4), 3 goods = +2, food 2 per turn (shortage costs). 8 turns. Moves: tira, relansez i,j, fin.
