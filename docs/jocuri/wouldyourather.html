Would You Rather

Screenshots and clip

Rules in short

Six dilemmas. For each, both players secretly pick their own answer (a or b) and guess the other's: moves aa, ab, ba, bb (first letter = your answer, second = your guess), then arat. Each correct guess scores 1; most points wins. Hidden choices.

Full rules page (in Romanian, on GitHub)

Play it

polytabletop play wouldyourather

Options

--you one|two|both|none
--level easy|normal|hard|expert
--lang en|ro|fr|it|es|pt|de|hu
--seed N
polytabletop --lang ro play wouldyourather --you both --level hard --seed 1
