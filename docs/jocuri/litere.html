Letters (words on a board)

Screenshots and clip

Rules in short

Original game on a 15×15 board with 97 tiles (2 blanks): you form words connected to the existing ones, with premium squares (# word ×3, + word ×2, x letter ×3, - letter ×2); +50 for using all 7 tiles. A small built-in Romanian dictionary (~4000 common words, without diacritics; absent words are rejected). Moves: casa@h8> (horizontal), casa@h8v (vertical), ca(s)a@h8> (blank in parentheses), schimb (whole hand), pas. The first word must cover h8. Hidden information: you only see your own hand.

Full rules page (in Romanian, on GitHub)
