Scopa (Italy)

Screenshots and clip

Rules in short

Italian 40-card deck (d c s b; ranks 1-7 F C R, values 1-10), 3 in hand, 4 on the table. Play a card: capture a card of equal value, otherwise a group with the same sum, otherwise leave it. Clearing the table is a scopa (1 point, not on the last card); scoring: cards, coins, settebello (7d), primiera, scope; match to 11. Hidden information: you only see Player 1's hand; the next deal is a forced move. Moves: 7d, 7d:3c+4b, 7d:7s, imparte, urmatoarea.
