Lighthouse Wardens (team missions, hidden roles)

Screenshots and clip

Rules in short

Five seats (you + 4 bots): three guards (one is the Eye, who knows the saboteurs) and two saboteurs (one is the Fox). Five missions with teams of 2, 3, 2, 3, 3: the leader proposes, everyone votes, the team members secretly choose success or sabotage (only saboteurs may sabotage). 3 successes = the guards, but the Fox may guess the Eye; 3 failures or 5 rejections = the saboteurs. Moves: echipa 1 3, da, nu, succes, sabotaj, tinta 4, masa.

Full rules page (in Romanian, on GitHub)
