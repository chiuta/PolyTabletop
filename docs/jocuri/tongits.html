Tong-its

Rules in short

52 cards, 12 each (the dealer 13 and starts without drawing). Turn: trage (draw) from the stock or take the discard if you meld it at once (ia 5d 5h 5c / ia sapaw 2), then lay down sets or runs (pune 3h 4h 5h), add to any meld (sapaw 6h 2) and discard (arunca Kd). Empty hand = Tongits, 3 pts; cheama (call; needs a meld on the table) → the opponent renunt (fold, 1 pt) or lupta (fight): fewer points in hand wins (tie: the fighter; if the caller loses the opponent gets 2); stock out: fewer points wins. First to 6 pts.
