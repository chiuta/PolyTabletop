Pontoon

Rules in short

Banker (role alternates every hand) against the player: Pontoon (ace + ten-value card) > Five Card Trick (5 cards ≤ 21) > 21 > closest to 21; the banker wins ties. The player: carte (twist), cumpar (buy: a face-down card, stake +10, only before any twist), stai (stick, from 15); the banker sticks from 17. Pontoon and Five Card Trick pay double. 20 hands with a stake of 10, whoever has more chips wins. Hidden information. Moves: carte, cumpar, stai, decontare.
