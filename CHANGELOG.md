# Changelog

## v0.9.2
- 240 playable games (was 171): 69 new. Împletitura, Cursa probelor and Itinerar; 16 role-playing adventures on the new `aventura` engine (Turnul Zorilor, Arhiva Abisală, Ruinele Cartografilor, Noapte de Catifea, Pădurea de Sticlă, Neon și Rune, Târgul Ciumat, Insula celor Două Luni, Hoții din Ceață, Crucea de Cenușă, Strada de Oțel, Ținutul Furtunilor, Epava din Inelul Șapte, Fiordul Gheții Negre, Mlaștina Strigoilor, Satul Lanternelor); 15 African and Middle-Eastern games (Oware, Bao, Seega, Yote, Hus, Tâb, Senet, Whot!, Chkobba, Ronda, Sholo Guti, Aadu Puli, Mangala, Dama, Okey); 12 Asian and Iranian games (Riichi Mahjong, Sic Bo, Ashta Chamma, Daifugo, Tiến lên, Big Two, Tongits, Pasur, Hokm, Pishti, Court Piece, Call Break); 12 North American games (Pinochle, Gin Rummy, Crazy Eights, Pitch, Bid Whist, All Fours, Conquián, Chicken Foot, Bunco, Farkle, Liar's Dice, Crokinole); 11 Latin American and Oceanian games (Chinchón, Burako, Escoba, Cuarenta, Generala, Parqués, Five Hundred, Pontoon, Two-up, Ancora, Mu Torere). Titles and short rules of all 69 are translated into the 8 languages; rules pages in `docs/reguli/` (the 16 adventures are still pending).
- Catalog: 1200 entries, the new games marked as implemented (239 implemented, 214 traditional planned, 475 own variants planned, 272 generic toolkit: 84 RPG systems, 93 miniatures, 95 collectible cards); `docs/CATALOG.md` and `docs/JOCURI.md` (240 games) regenerated; shell completions regenerated for 240 ids.
- GUI: embedded fonts (Cormorant Garamond, Lora, JetBrains Mono, DejaVu Sans fallback), the "Fără Frontiere" light/dark theme, persistent language setting, and playing cards, dice and dominoes drawn as vectors (French, Italian, Spanish and tarot decks).
- Site: the number of games is derived from the data (no hardcoded count); 240 game pages, each under 60 KB, also for games without screenshots or clip yet (the media section appears only when the files exist); the lightbox for screenshots (keyboard, swipe, full screen) with its test; the page markup is minified to stay under 60 KB.
- Site: twelve world rankings by category (Abstract, Classic, Family, Strategy, Party, Cooperative, Cards, Role-playing, Miniatures, Collectible cards, Children, Dice) in a second tab group "By category", each of exactly 100 distinct games, with a summary "X of 100 playable" that updates from the catalog status; game pages show the place in the category list; 8 languages.
- Site: nine top-100 lists instead of four (added Africa, Asia, North America, South America, Oceania; Antarctica has no list, noted in 8 languages). Regional rankings are editorial estimates.
- Catalog: 50 new traditional regional games with public rules (Oware, Bao, Seega, Yote, Hus, Tâb, Senet, Whot!, Chkobba, Ronda, Sholo Guti, Okey, Mangala, Riichi Mahjong, Daifugo, Pasur, Hokm, Pinochle, Gin Rummy, Conquián, Crokinole, Burako, Escoba, Cuarenta, Generala, Parqués, Two-up, Mu Torere and others), all now playable.
- Catalog: 12 categories of exactly 100 distinct entries each (Abstract, Classic, Family, Strategy, Party, Cooperative, Cards, Role-playing, Miniatures, Collectible cards, Children, Dice): 1200 entries in total; 959 new entries from the researched lists in `tools/data/cat100/` (merged by `tools/data/build_cat100.py` into `tools/data/data_cat100.py`). Duplicates were unified: Battleship/Bătălia navală, Guess Who?/Cine e?, Ludo/Mensch ärgere dich nicht/Nu te supăra, frate, Moara/Țintar, Santase/Șase-zeci și șase, Remi/Rummy (13 cărți), the three Werewolf entries; the old names keep resolving in the regional rankings. Dice games moved to the new Dice category (Yahtzee, Generala, Sic Bo, Bunco, Farkle, Liar's Dice, Barbut, Crown and Anchor).
- Docs: `docs/reguli/INDEX.md` (games 1–120) and `INDEX2.md` (121–240) generated by `tools/gen_reguli_index.py`; READMEs in 8 languages, man page and architecture notes updated to 240 games.

## v0.9.1
- Help/`rules` texts aligned with the code (no gameplay changes): pachisi (cowrie table: none open = 25, 1 = 10, 5 = 5; graces 6/10/25), roata (real branch labels `avansez facultate|ucenicie|afacere|salariu`), reverdire (forced move `generatie`), fanorona (every prefix of a capture chain is a separate move; the chosen move ends the turn), tcg_cerneala (`pesca` is a forced move every turn; P1 draws no card on the first turn), tinuturi (24 tiles drawn from the set of 48), portret (`verdict corect` / `verdict gresit` given by the opponent). Titles/rules in all 8 languages and `docs/reguli/*.md` updated.
- magistrale: module header now says 42 routes (38 + 4 extra), like the code and tests.
- schita: fixed the unsubstituted placeholder in the hints for `patru_picioare` / `doua_picioare`; new test rejects `_`, `{`, `}` in any hint text.
- New tests: pachisi rules vs cowry table, roata rules vs real move labels, reverdire/fanorona/tcg_cerneala/tinuturi/portret rules vs moves.

## v0.9
- 171 playable games (201 catalog entries).
- Interface in 8 languages (en ro fr it es pt de hu) for the CLI and the GUI: `--lang`, `POLYTABLETOP_LANG`; READMEs and install guides in all 8.
- AI levels `--level easy|normal|hard|expert` (`POLYTABLETOP_LEVEL`).
- `polytabletop arena`, `bench`, `ladder` (AI against AI); `host` / `join` for two-player network play; `--record`, `replay`, `export` (pgn for chess, text, json) and `import-pgn`.
- `install.sh` (installs the `ptt` alias), man page, shell completions, Dockerfile; CI and release workflows on Ubuntu 26.04.
- Titles and rules of all 171 games translated into the 8 languages (text inside the games is still Romanian).
